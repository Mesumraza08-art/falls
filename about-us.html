About Fan Travel

Dedicated to Making Your Travel Unforgettable.

A TICO-certified travel agency built on one simple idea: listen first, then plan the trip around the traveller.

Our quest is to manage your travel needs and fulfil vacation dreams through personalised, world-class service.

Our founder

Faneeza Esoof

Founder & CEO

I was born in the enchanted land of Guyana, South America. My first journey came at the age of twelve, when my family took our first European vacation. I marvelled at the majestic mountain ranges of Switzerland, the charming villages of Italy and the vibrant culture of Germany, and that trip ignited my passion for travel and exploration.

In 2002, after moving to Canada, I returned to the world of travel. I have worked with all the major airline carriers and cruise companies to give my clients the best possible options.

Snorkelling in Hawaii, ziplining in Jamaica, swimming with the dolphins in the Bahamas and hiking Turtle Mountain in Guyana are just a few of the adventures I have experienced, and I bring that first-hand knowledge to every trip I plan.
