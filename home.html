Meet your travel specialist

Faneeza Esoof

Founder & CEO, Fan Travel Service

Born in Guyana, Faneeza took her first journey at twelve, on a family trip through the mountains of Switzerland, the villages of Italy and the culture of Germany. That trip started a lifelong passion for exploring.

After moving to Canada in 2002 she returned to the world of travel, working with all the major airlines and cruise companies to find the best options for her clients. Today she is a destination wedding and honeymoon specialist covering all sun destinations, and a certified Jamaica, RIU, Hawaii and Sandals specialist.

Snorkelling in Hawaii, ziplining in Jamaica, swimming with dolphins in the Bahamas and hiking Turtle Mountain in Guyana are a few of her own adventures.
